Arrival in Paradise

🌅 Reach Shangarh 🚿 Freshen Up 🏡 Check-in at Woodzo ☕ Hot Chai
01

Shangarh Meadows

One of the most beautiful meadows in the Himalayas. A vast carpet of green stretching to the edge of the world, with a centuries-old wooden temple at its heart. Walk barefoot, breathe deep, and let the mountains hold you.

🌿 Meadow Walk 📸 Photography 🏛 Ancient Temple 🚁 Drone Shots
02

Barshangarh Waterfall

Hidden in the forest, this waterfall descends through moss-covered rocks into a crystal pool. The mist on your face, the roar of water, the absolute green everywhere — it's nature at its most raw and beautiful.

💧 Waterfall 🌲 Forest Walk 📸 Photography 🔊 Nature Sounds
03

Shangchul Mahadev Temple 🔔

A sacred wooden temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, adorned with exquisite Himachali wood carvings. Standing here, surrounded by deodar trees and silence, you feel the weight of centuries and the lightness of peace.

🕉 Sacred Site 🪵 Wood Carvings 🧘 Peaceful 📜 History
